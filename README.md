# Retail Sales Analysis & Excel Dashboard

I built this project to practice cleaning, analyzing, and visualizing retail sales data in Excel.

## What I Did 

- Cleaned the dataset and removed duplicate records
- Created PivotTables to analyze sales
- Built charts to visualize sales trends
- Created an interactive dashboard using slicers
- Analyzed sales by category, region, segment, year, and product

## Project Results

- 9,800 original records
- 9,792 records after cleaning
- 4,922 unique orders
- 793 unique customers
- $2.26M in total sales

## Key Findings

- Technology had the highest category sales at $827K
- The West had the highest regional sales at $710K
- Consumer customers generated $1.15M in sales
- Sales increased from $460K in 2016 to $721K in 2018
- The Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the top-selling product at $61.6K
